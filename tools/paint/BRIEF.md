# Gear brief: Centurion set + BLU outfit (Hume male)

Every piece is described in words first and then painted new from these words.
Nothing is filtered or fixed from the old texture; the old texture only tells us
where each object sits on the locked UV layout, where the see-through cut-outs
are, and where the mirror seams run.

## Style (all pieces)
- Bright, over-sold cel colour: 3 flat tones per material (shadow, base, light) plus
  a white glint. Crisp inked edges inside each piece, none on mirror seams.
- Real surfaces on top of the colour:
  - Steel: brushed grain, fine scratches, small dents, a hard tilted horizon
    reflection, lit top/left bevel, dark lower rim, one diagonal glint.
  - Leather: pebbled grain, creases, scuffed lighter edges, stitch holes.
  - Cloth: twill weave and threads (indigo); coarse canvas weave (gloves).
- Plating is always stacked: rows overlap the row below, the lower plate sits in
  the shadow of the lap, the overlapping edge is rolled and catches the light.
- Mirror seams: anything crossing one is drawn past it so it mirrors into one
  whole shape; a centre line is kept where the original had one.
- Cut-outs stay exactly where the original has them, with a crisp ink edge.
- Blue (indigo) belongs to the Centurion cloth only. The Battle Gloves and
  Bounding Boots have no blue at all: they are cloth and leather.

Palette: steel #5c6788/#b7c3dc/#e8eef9; leather #5a2f1c/#9a5a32/#c98a54,
stitching #e7c58e; indigo cloth #1c1f45/#2f3570/#4a539c; ink #140e24.

## Centurion's Scale Mail (body)
- **Neck ring**: a rolled leather collar round the neck, bright crown, two rows of
  stitching running round it, dark inside the opening.
- **Collar roll**: indigo cloth rolled over a curved neckline, lit along its crown.
- **Neck band**: a curved leather band following the neckline arch, stitched on both
  edges, studs where it meets the shoulder straps.
- **Yoke**: leather under the shoulder straps either side of the neck opening.
- **Cow emblem**: on the front shoulder strap, centred on the chest seam, a small
  brass medallion with a raised cow's head (ivory horns and muzzle, ears, eyes,
  nostrils, a dark brow spot).
- **Chest**: six rows of steel shield scales in an offset brick pattern, each row
  lapping the one below, between riveted leather side straps.
- **Centre panel**: indigo cloth with a domed top following the neck opening, two
  tall piped panels and a leather ladder.
- **Abdomen and back skirt**: three overlapping steel lames each side in a leather
  frame; the lames meet on the mirror seam with a thin centre line.
- **Hip tabs**: dark leather.
- **Rear skirt**: five overlapping lames held by riveted vertical leather straps.
- **Belt**: one solid leather band of stitched blocks with four rivets.
- **Pauldrons**: dark steel top bars with a rivet, hanging pointed steel plates
  (points exactly where the original cut-outs put them), a dark leather hinge strap.

## Centurion's Cuisses (legs)
- **Trousers**: indigo cloth, long vertical folds, a darker gusset fold.
- **Thigh strap**: leather, stitched both edges, two steel frame buckles; on the
  outside it runs down toward the knee.
- **Tasset**: on the outer thigh, three rows of four overlapping steel lames on a
  dark leather backing, two rivets at the top corners.
- **Inner panels**: slate-blue cloth either side of the tasset.
- **Knee strap**: stitched leather above the knee; dark indigo cuff below it.
- **Knee cop**: steel plate with a rolled rim and two rivets in a stitched leather
  surround (mirrored top to bottom into one plate).

## Battle Gloves (hands): cloth, brown, no blue
- **Hand**: brown wrapped canvas, banded by the wraps.
- **Finger seams**: raised stitched canvas piping in U shapes over the fingers.
- **Cuff**: deep olive quilted canvas, vertical gathers, its curved lower edge
  bound with light canvas piping.
- **Wrist**: a light canvas welt across, leather band with two leather stud tabs.
- **Inside**: dark.

## Bounding Boots (feet): leather, no blue
- **Shaft**: saddle leather, creases, a dark top rim with stitching, a raised
  stitched seam across the shin, four rivets, a stitched seam down the side.
- **Toe cap and heel**: light tan leather, stitched, dark soles.
- **Knee tab**: tan leather with a rounded foot and two rivets.
- **Wrap band**: dark leather strips wound round below the cuff.
- **Buckle**: steel ladder buckle (two slots) on tan leather with a rivet.

## Centurion's Visor (head)
- **Brow band**: across the forehead, two bright steel plates (outer plate with a
  stepped lower edge and a rivet, square centre plate) set in dark leather, two
  narrow steel bars between them, and a dark steel end plate with a rivet.
- **Cheek guards**: L-shaped steel plates hanging beside the face.
- **Chin strap**: dark leather, stitched, running down each side and under the
  chin, joined by a stitched diagonal seam.

## Centurion's Finger Gauntlets (hands)
- **Cuff**: leather band at the wrist with a long steel plate and rivet across it.
- **Wrist lames**: two rows of steel plates on the back of the wrist, the lower row
  pointed like fingers (points where the cut-outs are), overlapping the upper row,
  on dark steel bands.
- **Glove**: dark leather glove, darker palm, a leather strap across the knuckles.

## Centurion's Greaves (feet)
- **Leg harness**: a grid of leather straps (vertical straps, a broad strap round
  the top of the shin with a steel buckle and a rivet) holding dark padded panels.
- **Shin plates**: steel winged plates down the front, overlapping, with rivets
  (points where the cut-outs are).
- **Calf**: plain brown leather at the back, creased.

## Centurion's Sword
- **Grip**: dark leather wrap with steel end bands.
- **Pommel**: faceted steel diamond.
- **Crossguard**: steel with dark engraved channels.
- **Blade**: bright steel; a darker fuller down the flat, a polished bevelled edge,
  hard reflection and glint.

## Centurion's Axe
- **Head**: blued dark steel (navy) faces with a hard reflection and a bright
  ground edge.
- **Ornament**: gold filigree scrollwork on dark steel around a central gem.
- **Haft**: dark wood/leather wrap with gold bands; a gold spike.
